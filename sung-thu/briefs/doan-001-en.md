Write the opening segment of the novel, about 2,000 Chinese characters, in Chinese.

SCENE
The 18th, from 6:40 to 8:52 in the morning. 「第七收容站」 (Shelter No. 7), at the end of 窑厂路 in the old 老窑 district on the south side of 宁川 — kennel row A and the door of the duty room.

许野, 22, is the last employee left at the shelter. He is giving a final meal to 27 mutated beasts whose public notice period (公示期) runs out at 9:00 this morning. At exactly 9:00 the white truck from 「净城生物」 will come and haul all 27 away to be destroyed (无害化处理).

Cage 17 holds 大黄: a yellow-coated mutt (田园犬) with a floppy left ear, caught by city officers (城管) on 窑厂路 at 9:00 a.m. seven days ago.

This morning, when 许野 looks into cage 17, his talent 「鉴定」 shows six lines for the first time instead of the four it has shown for two years — two new lines: 种族异能 and 个体天赋. He walks the whole row to check; every beast now shows six lines. Of the 27, almost all have only ordinary talents; one in cage 9 is worth a second look; 大黄 in cage 17 is worth betting his only contract slot on.

Before 8:52 he scans the shelter's payment code to pay the 200-yuan adoption fee, then crouches in the cage and forms a contract with 大黄.

FACTS OF THIS WORLD (get them right; no need to state them all)
- The 天裂 opened in the sky 52 years ago; this is year 52 of the 兽潮 era. 宁川 has 12 million people and three rings of walls; outside the walls is the 野区.
- Life inside the walls is like a city today: phones, pay-by-QR, food delivery on e-bikes.
- At 16 people awaken; the 御兽协会 grades each person's 本命天赋 from S to E and prints it on their 御兽师 card.
- A 见习御兽师 has exactly one contract slot. Once it is used, a second slot only comes with promotion to 中级御兽师, which takes years.
- City rule on stray beasts: every ownerless beast inside the walls goes to a shelter; the 公示期 is 7×24 hours, counted from the time it was registered in; if no one adopts it by then, 净城生物 takes it away to be destroyed.
- 开元宠业 sells 宠兽盲盒 (beast blind boxes) at 1,288 yuan each; beasts that fail their standard are labelled 「残次」 and dumped at shelters, with 开元's yellow label on the cage.
- An 精英级 beast egg costs about 32,000 yuan on the market.
- The district has given notice: the shelter closes on the 30th of this month.

THINGS AT HAND
A clipboard with the list of the 27 expiring beasts (printed sheet with columns for cage number, species, and time registered in); a bucket of dry dog food and an aluminium scoop; a ring of cage keys on his belt; 许野's 御兽师 card (on the back: 本命天赋 E级「鉴定」, 契约位 0/1); his phone (balance 1,340 yuan); the shelter's payment QR code taped to the duty-room door.

How a contract works: the 御兽师 lays a palm on the beast's forehead; if the beast does not resist, a contract mark (契约纹) appears on the back of the person's hand, and the card's slot line changes to 契约位 1/1.

PANELS
大黄's panel prints in full, in the format the SI sets: 种族 田园犬; 属性 土; 种族等级 普通级; 生命等级 6级; 种族异能 撕咬; two 个体天赋 — 看家 (普通天赋: all abilities rise slightly while on its own territory) and 天狗血脉 (传说天赋: a very thin strand of 天狗 blood sleeps in its body; once conditions are met it can evolve back toward its ancestor).

Cage 9 holds a 开元 「残次」 雪绒狐 with black fur (the standard for this breed is pure white); its second talent is 霜心 (稀有天赋: the cold inside its body has condensed into a core).

All other beasts get only a count or a passing glance. No full panel prints for anyone except 大黄.

WHERE EACH PERSON IS RIGHT NOW
许野 has just come off the night shift; he lives in the shelter's duty room. In the past seven days he has phoned every would-be adopter on file; nobody came. Two years a 见习御兽师, zero contracts: good beasts are beyond his money, and he would not spend his only slot on a poor one.

For seven days, at every feeding time, 大黄 has been sitting at the cage door, waiting.

Around 8:30, 蔡德旺, the 净城生物 driver, phones to confirm the count and his arrival time.

THREE THINGS IN EACH SPEAKER'S HEAD (no lines are scripted)
许野 — wants: to keep the dog, and to find out what else his eyes can see now; won't say aloud: that 「鉴定」 showed two extra lines today; hands: holding the clipboard and the key ring.
蔡德旺 (on the phone) — wants: a full load of 27 and an early finish; won't say aloud: the company pays him per head; hands: on the steering wheel, a radio opera playing in his earpiece.

MUST HAPPEN
The contract is complete before 8:52; 许野's card reads 契约位 1/1; 26 names remain on the list.

STOP AT
8:52 — the truck's horn sounds outside the iron gate; 许野 shoves the card into his pocket and walks toward the gate with the clipboard.

PEOPLE PRESENT
许野 — 22, contract worker at the shelter, 见习御兽师 for two years, no contracts; orange work jacket with faded 「收容站」 lettering, three adhesive bandages on the back of his hand.
大黄 — yellow-coated 田园犬, floppy left ear, cage 17.
蔡德旺 — 50, 净城生物 truck driver, yellow rubber gloves, radio opera in his earpiece.

FORMS OF ADDRESS
许野 calls 蔡德旺 「蔡师傅」 and refers to himself as 「我」; 蔡德旺 calls 许野 「小许」; 许野 calls the dog 「大黄」.

NUMBERS FOR THIS SEGMENT
27 beasts expire at 9:00 a.m.; 净城生物 arrives at 9:00; the shelter closes on the 30th (today is the 18th); balance 1,340 yuan, adoption fee 200 yuan each; contract slot 0/1 before, 1/1 after; 精英级 beast egg about 32,000 yuan; 开元 blind box 1,288 yuan.
