Defining the Genre's Scope

I'm currently working to pin down the specific parameters of this writing endeavor. I've broken down the request, focusing on the genre (light-hearted beast-taming web novel), and the stylistic considerations, such as the point of view (third-person limited) and the inner voice that I need to project (Lin Yi). Clarifying The Constraints

I've been meticulously cataloging the stylistic restrictions for this project. No mental reporting verbs or quote marks on inner monologue! Literary clichés are out, and physical reactions are to be replaced with internal outbursts. I'm focusing on minimal speech tags and short, punchy paragraphs. First mentions of proper terms will be in 「」 brackets, and scene changes will be marked with lines of dots.
